# Duty Calendar

Clean, professional duty tracking with a calendar view, month overview, and quick entry.

## Summary
- **Start date:** May 2, 2026
- **Days off:** May 10, May 13, May 20

## Highlights
- Month overview cards with duty/off totals and next off day
- Quick entry form with notes, hours, and location
- Calendar grid with status badges and today highlight
- Entries saved locally in the browser
- One-click reset to default entries

## Files
- `index.html` – Main duty calendar UI.
- `styles.css` – Clean, professional styling.
- `script.js` – Calendar logic + local storage.
- `calendar.md` – Printable calendar view for May 2026 with days off highlighted.
